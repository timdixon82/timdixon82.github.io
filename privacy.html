1. Who is responsible for your information

Tim Dixon is responsible for the personal information processed through Son of Anton. Contact: soa@dixon-net.com.

2. Scope

This policy explains how Son of Anton, including its Discord bot, commands, integrations, website, and related services, collects, uses, stores, and shares information.

3. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Discord identifiers: user ID, server ID, channel ID, role ID, command ID, and message ID;
  • Discord profile information: username, display name, avatar, and server membership information made available through Discord;
  • Configuration: settings selected by administrators, including allowed users, channels, roles, and integration options;
  • Command and message data: command names, parameters, and message content only where required by an enabled feature;
  • Technical data: timestamps, error details, diagnostic logs, IP address where collected by hosting infrastructure, and basic browser information for website visitors;
  • Support communications: information you provide when requesting support or contacting us.

4. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • provide requested bot commands and integrations;
  • apply server settings and access controls;
  • maintain security, prevent abuse, and investigate faults;
  • operate, troubleshoot, and improve the Service;
  • respond to support requests; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

5. Legal bases

Where UK or European data protection law applies, we rely on one or more appropriate legal bases, including performance of a contract, legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, compliance with legal obligations, and consent where specifically requested.

6. Message content and privileged intents

The Service does not read or store general message content unless a feature requires it and the necessary Discord permissions or intents have been enabled. The application only accesses message content where required to fulfil a command initiated by the owner. Message content is not routinely stored. Temporary processing may occur in memory while a request is handled, and diagnostic logs do not intentionally include message content except where necessary to troubleshoot an error.

7. Sharing of information

We may share limited information with:

  • Discord, as necessary to operate through Discord;
  • hosting, database, monitoring, or integration providers acting on our behalf;
  • professional advisers or authorities where legally required; and
  • a successor organisation if the Service is transferred, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal information.

8. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above. Diagnostic logs are retained for up to 30 days. Configuration data is retained while the application is in use. Support correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to resolve the issue. Data may be retained longer where required by law, needed to resolve disputes, or necessary to protect the Service.

9. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including access controls, restricted permissions, software updates, and secure hosting practices. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

10. International transfers

Discord and other providers may process information outside your country. Where required, we use or rely on recognised safeguards for international data transfers.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability of your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, email soa@dixon-net.com. We may need to verify your identity and identify the relevant Discord user or server.

12. Deleting your data

You may request deletion by contacting soa@dixon-net.com and providing your Discord user ID and any relevant server ID. Removing the bot from a server stops future access to that server but may not automatically delete retained configuration or logs.

13. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone who is not permitted to use Discord under Discord’s rules or applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children outside the permitted use of the Service.

14. Cookies and website hosting

These legal pages do not intentionally set cookies. The hosting provider may process technical logs or apply essential security features. No analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies are intentionally used.

15. Complaints

Please contact us first at soa@dixon-net.com. Where UK data protection law applies, you may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Service, legal requirements, or processing practices change. The effective date at the top will show when it was last revised.

17. Contact
